Mid Lane Wave Management Guide

Wave Management for Mid Lane

Learn how to control minion waves to gain advantages.

Slow Push

  • Last hit only
  • Builds up a large wave
  • Dive opponent under turret

Fast Push

  • Clear wave quickly
  • Freeze near your turret
  • Deny enemy CS

Freeze

  • Keep wave just outside turret
  • Safe farming
  • Easy gank setup
